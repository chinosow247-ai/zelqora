# Zelqora store metadata checklist

Prepare before submission:
- App name: Zelqora
- App icon and adaptive icon
- Splash screen
- Screenshots for supported phone sizes
- Short and full descriptions
- Support URL
- Privacy Policy URL
- Terms/Community Guidelines URL
- Content rating answers
- Data safety/privacy declarations
- Age rating
- Contact/support email
- Developer/merchant legal information
- Test account for store reviewers if required

Do not submit until real URLs and legal contact information have replaced placeholders.
