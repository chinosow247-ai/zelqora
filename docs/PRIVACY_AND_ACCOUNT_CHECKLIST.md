# Privacy and account checklist

- Finalize Privacy Policy with the real legal/business contact.
- Finalize Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
- Explain what profile, message, video and device-token data is collected.
- Provide account deletion and data-deletion handling.
- Provide a way to correct account information.
- Define retention periods for inactive accounts, reports and logs.
- Do not expose email addresses unnecessarily in public profiles.
- Use HTTPS everywhere in production.
- Rotate secrets and never commit `.env` files.
